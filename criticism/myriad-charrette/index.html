Myriad Design Charrette on the Preservation of Complex Digital-Born Artworks

August 2, 2022


After being initially invited to submit my name as part of a National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH) grant being led by Myriad Consulting in 2020, I had the honor of participating in a design charrette that occurred as part of the grant's early actualization.

I count myself fortunate to have worked so closely with both artists and archive maintainers from prestigious institutions across the US to brainstorm, share experiences, and work towards a solution to the problem of how to faithfully, usefully, and efficiently preserve complex digital-born pieces of artwork.

I was also the official photographer for the event.
