Anthelion

2020–2025


A 5-minute animated film, "Anthelion" tells the story of a woman's walk through the snow. A quiet rumination on dependence and independence, this story makes use of a charcoal style brush and maintains a black and white palette throughout.

It has been shown at the Staten Island Wavestock Festival, at Octopus Marquee, and at Like It Film Fest.

A sibling film, "The Well," is in the works and is expected to be completed later this year.
