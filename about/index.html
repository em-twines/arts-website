Emily Delbridge (Twines) is an interdisciplinary performance and technology artist, writer, and software engineer with a passion for storytelling through new and challenging media. She has experience directing, writing, programming, and designing interactive theatrical elements in NYC, where she earned her MA in Theatre History and Criticism as well her MFA in Performance and Interactive Media Arts.

Works include writing for "The House," a short film by renowned director Cyril Teste and team, as well as work on "I heard an echo," a collaborative installation at The Weeksville Heritage Center in Brooklyn, for which she provided video and interactivity elements through use of Max MSP, a Kinect, and an Arduino set-up.

Her COVID project, a narrative, video-art driven video game called LookingGlass, was streamed / played via The Living Gallery, OyeDrum literary magazine, ReclaimFutures' festival, and EdgeCut.

She also recently debuted a short animated film, "Anthelion," at the Wavestock Film Festival on Staten Island.

After 11 years in NYC, Emily is exploring Nashville in her home state of TN, where she lives with her boyfriend and her cat, Hachi.
