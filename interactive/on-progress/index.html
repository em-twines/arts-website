'On Progress' With Professor Stewart Orwell Teric III

March 12, 2019


The piece features a drag character—a pitch-shifted "vaping professor" version of the artist—delivering a lecture on progress from Aristotle to Kuhn. As the lecture intensifies, video projection displays a pre-recorded version progressively distorted through MaxMSP with loops and echoes. The live performer becomes increasingly animated, eventually diverging to tend a fallen potted plant while the projected professor continues alone and the artist attempts to recover spilled soil.

"A commentary on western progress narratives and the masturbatory potential of endless theoretical debate, this variety show piece challenged the audience to address the here and now, the ever-present environmental crisis, and their relationship to their homes."
