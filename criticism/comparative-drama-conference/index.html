Comparative Drama Conference

April 1, 2016


Presentation of a paper titled "Puppet Dramaturgy: Curated vs. Created Work at St. Ann's Warehouse."

The paper explored whether creations from the St. Ann's puppet lab fundamentally differ from invited works, seeking to compare these two bodies of work to identify potential insights.
